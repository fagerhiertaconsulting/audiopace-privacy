# Privacy Policy for AudioPace

Last updated: October 2026

AudioPace ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, processing, and storage.

### 1. No Collection of Personal Information
AudioPace does not collect, track, transmit, or sell any personal information, browsing history, keystrokes, IP addresses, or viewing habits.

### 2. Local Media Processing
All audio analysis, silence detection, dynamic playback rate adjustments, and speech clarity compression occur strictly locally in your browser via the standard Web Audio API and HTMLMediaElement. No audio streams, video content, or media metadata are ever recorded, uploaded, or transmitted to any external server.

### 3. Local Storage Usage
AudioPace uses the browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) solely on your device to:
- Persist your personal playback preferences (custom speeds, sensitivity thresholds, and creator channel profiles).
- Store an anonymous, offline tally of estimated time saved.
- Cache subscription license status for offline validation.

None of this data leaves your local device.

### 4. Payments and Licensing
AudioPace uses [ExtensionPay](https://extensionpay.com) and [Stripe](https://stripe.com/privacy) for processing voluntary upgrades to AudioPace Pro. Payment transactions and billing information are handled directly and securely by Stripe in compliance with PCI-DSS standards. AudioPace does not have access to, nor does it store or process, credit card numbers or financial credentials. An anonymous installation identifier is used with ExtensionPay solely to verify license status.

### 5. Changes to This Policy
If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be posted here with an updated revision date.
